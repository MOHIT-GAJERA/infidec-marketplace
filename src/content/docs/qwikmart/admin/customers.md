## Customers

**Customers → Manage Customers**: search by name, e-mail or phone; see orders count, total spent and wallet balance. Open a customer to view orders, addresses, wallet history and referrals, or to **block** the account (blocked users are signed out of the apps).

Customers create their own accounts with phone OTP or e-mail in the app or on the website. Guests can browse and fill a cart before logging in — the cart is kept when they sign up.

## Wallet adjustments

From a customer → **Wallet → Adjust**: credit (e.g. goodwill, cashback) or debit an amount with a note. Every change is recorded in **Wallet & Withdrawals → Wallet Transactions** — balances can never be edited directly.

## Withdrawal requests

Customers (and riders) can request money from their wallet to their bank account if withdrawals are enabled.

**Wallet & Withdrawals → Withdrawal Requests** → open a request → transfer the money from your bank → **Approve** and upload the receipt / reference, or **Reject** with a remark (the amount returns to the wallet).

![Withdrawals](/docs/qwikmart/images/admin-withdrawals.jpg "Withdrawal Requests")

## Referrals

**Customers → Referrals** shows who invited whom and whether the reward was released (after the referred customer's first qualifying order). Configure rewards in [Wallet, Referral & Delivery](doc:admin/wallet-referral-delivery).

## Account deletion

Customers can delete their account in the app (**Profile → Delete account**). Their profile is anonymised and the login deleted; order records are kept for accounting.
