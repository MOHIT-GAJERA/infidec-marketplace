Riders (delivery boys) register themselves in the **Delivery Boy App**; you review their documents and approve them. You can also add riders yourself.

![Delivery boys](/docs/qwikmart/images/admin-delivery-boys.jpg "Admin → Delivery Boys")

## Approve a registration

:::steps
### Rider registers
In the app the rider enters name, phone, date of birth, country and **zone**, address, vehicle type and number, uploads **driving licence** and **ID proof**, and adds bank details for payouts. Status: *Pending*.

### Review
**Delivery Boys → Pending Approvals** → open the request, check the documents.

### Approve or reject
**Approve** (confirm the zone) — the rider is notified and can go online. **Reject** with a remark — the rider sees the reason.
:::

## Manage riders

**Delivery Boys → Manage**: edit details, zone, vehicle, commission override, block / unblock, see rating, online status and current location.

## Commission and earnings

- Default commission per delivered order: **System Settings → Delivery** (fixed amount or percent). Override per rider in the rider's profile.
- Every delivered order creates an earning entry. **Earnings/Payouts** lists them; pay riders and record the payout.

## Cash collection (COD)

When a rider delivers a COD order the cash amount is added to their **cash in hand**. **Cash Collection** shows each rider's balance; record the amount when the rider deposits cash with you. A rider cannot delete their account while holding cash.

## Return pickups

Approved returns can be assigned to a rider for pickup — see [Returns & Refunds](doc:admin/returns).

## Rider web panel

Riders can also use the browser panel at `/delivery/login` with the same login as the app.
