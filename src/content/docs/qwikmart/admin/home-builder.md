The **Home Builder** controls what customers see on the home screen of the **Customer App** and the **website**. Each zone has two layouts: **Quick** and **eCommerce** (customers switch with the toggle at the top of the home screen).

![Home Builder](/docs/qwikmart/images/admin-home-builder.jpg "Admin → Home Builder")

## Section types

| Section | What it shows |
|---|---|
| `banner_slider` | Swipeable banners (image + link to a category, product or URL) |
| `category_section` | A row or grid of categories |
| `product_slider` | A horizontal list of products: hand-picked, a category, best sellers, newest or biggest discount |
| `top_brands` | Brand logos |
| `grid_banner` | 2–4 banners in a grid |
| `title_image` | A full-width image with a title |
| `heading_text` | A heading / text block |

## Build a layout

:::steps
### Choose zone and mode
**Home Builder → Quick Layouts** (or **eCommerce Layouts**) → choose the zone.

### Add sections
Click **Add section**, pick a type and fill its content. Upload banners in **Banners** first, or directly in the section.

### Reorder
Drag sections into the order you want. Use the device preview on the right to check the result.

### Header
Set the header colour shown behind the search bar for this layout.

### Publish
Click **Publish**. Until then your changes are a **draft** — customers keep seeing the published version.
:::

:::note Drafts are safe
Editing never changes the live home screen. Only **Publish** copies the draft into the public layout, so you can prepare a festival layout in advance.
:::

## Tips

- Keep the first screen light: one banner slider, one category row and one product slider.
- Banner size: 1200 × 500 px (website) works on the app too; keep text away from the edges.
- Use product sliders based on a category or "best sellers" — they stay fresh without manual work.
