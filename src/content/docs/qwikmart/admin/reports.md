---
title: Reports
description: Sales, product, tax, store and delivery boy reports with filters and CSV export.
---

**Admin → Reports**

![Reports](/docs/qwikmart/images/admin-reports.jpg "Admin → Reports")

| Report | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Sales Report** | Orders and revenue by day, with totals, discounts, delivery fees and tax |
| **Product Sales** | Best-selling products and variants, quantity and revenue |
| **Tax Report** | Tax collected per rate — for GST filing |
| **Store Report** | Orders and revenue per store, commission |
| **Delivery Boy Report** | Deliveries, earnings and cash collected per rider |
| **Quick vs eCommerce** | Share of orders and revenue by fulfilment mode |

Every report has a date range and zone/store filters and can be **exported to CSV**. The dashboard uses the same daily statistics, updated automatically as orders change.
