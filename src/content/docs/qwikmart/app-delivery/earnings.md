| Screen | What the rider sees |
|---|---|
| **Home dashboard** | Today's deliveries and earnings, cash in hand, rating, online switch |
| **Earnings** | One entry per delivered order (commission: fixed or % — set in Admin → System Settings → Delivery or per rider) |
| **Cash** | COD cash collected and deposits recorded by the admin (Admin → Cash Collection) |
| **Wallet** | Balance, transactions, **withdraw** to the bank account given at registration |

Withdrawal requests are approved in **Admin → Wallet & Withdrawals → Withdrawal Requests**; the rider sees the status and receipt/remark.

Riders also have **Support chat**, **Notifications** and **Profile** (change password, language, delete account).
