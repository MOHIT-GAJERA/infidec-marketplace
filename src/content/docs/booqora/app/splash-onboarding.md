When the Mobile App starts, the user sees up to four stages:

1. **Native splash**: drawn by Android or iOS while Flutter starts. Generated by `flutter_native_splash`.
2. **Animated splash** (`/splash`): the logo and wordmark animate in while the app checks the sign-in state.
3. **Onboarding** (`/onboarding`): four pages, shown only on the first launch.
4. **Intro** (`/intro`): *Create free account* or *I already have an account*.

All Flutter screens for stages 2–4 live in `lib/features/welcome/welcome_pages.dart`. The illustrations are in `lib/features/welcome/illustrations.dart`.

## Native splash

The configuration is at the bottom of `pubspec.yaml`:

```yaml title="booqora_mobile/pubspec.yaml"
flutter_native_splash:
  color: "#1C0938"
  image: assets/brand/splash_mark.png
  android_12:
    color: "#1C0938"
    image: assets/brand/splash_android12.png
    icon_background_color: "#1C0938"
  ios: true
  android: true
  web: false
  fullscreen: false
```

Replace the images in `assets/brand/`, change `color` to your background colour, then regenerate:

```bash title="Terminal"
cd booqora_mobile
dart run flutter_native_splash:create
```

This rewrites the Android `drawable*` and `values*` resources and the iOS `LaunchImage` and `LaunchBackground` assets. Run it again every time you change the images or colours.

:::note Android 12 and newer
Android 12+ ignores the normal splash image and always shows an **icon-style** splash: the image is masked to a circle in the centre of the screen. That is why there is a separate `splash_android12.png` (1152 × 1152). Keep your logo inside the central circle, about two-thirds of the image width, with transparent padding. Otherwise the edges are cut off.
:::

## Animated splash

`SplashPage` in `welcome_pages.dart` draws an aurora background (`AuroraBackground`), a pulsing ring, the `logo_mark.png` image and the wordmark letter by letter, then the tagline.

| What | Where in `welcome_pages.dart` |
|---|---|
| Wordmark | `const word = 'Booqora';` |
| Tagline | `'Rental management, beautifully simple'` |
| Minimum display time | `Future.delayed(const Duration(milliseconds: 2600))` |
| Background colours | `AuroraBackground` and `AppColors.ink` / `AppColors.heroGradient` in `lib/core/theme.dart` |

The splash waits at least 2.6 seconds **and** until Firebase reports the sign-in state. Then it routes:

- signed in with a workspace → `/home`;
- signed in without a workspace → `/welcome` (workspace onboarding);
- signed out and onboarding already seen → `/intro`;
- signed out, first launch → `/onboarding`.

Shorten the 2600 ms if you prefer a faster start. The animations use `flutter_animate`; delays such as `500 + i * 70` are in milliseconds.

## Onboarding pages

![Onboarding 1](/docs/booqora/images/app-onboarding-1.webp "Bookings")
![Onboarding 2](/docs/booqora/images/app-onboarding-2.webp "Inventory")
![Onboarding 3](/docs/booqora/images/app-onboarding-3.webp "Payments")
![Onboarding 4](/docs/booqora/images/app-onboarding-4.webp "Online store")

The pages are a list of `_Slide` entries: illustration, eyebrow, title, body text and icon.

```dart title="lib/features/welcome/welcome_pages.dart"
const _slides = [
  _Slide(BookingIllustration(), 'BOOKINGS', 'Every booking,\nperfectly on time',
      'Live availability, smart pricing and a calendar that shows exactly what goes out and comes back.',
      Icons.event_available_rounded),
  // INVENTORY, PAYMENTS, ONLINE STORE …
];
```

- **Edit text**: change the strings. `\n` forces a line break in the title.
- **Remove a page**: delete its `_Slide`. The page indicator (`smooth_page_indicator`) and the progress ring adapt automatically.
- **Add a page**: add a `_Slide`. Reuse an existing illustration or build your own widget in `illustrations.dart` (`BookingIllustration`, `InventoryIllustration`, `PaymentsIllustration`, `StoreIllustration`). They are drawn in code, so there are no image files to replace.

*Skip* and the final *Get started* button both call `OnboardingFlag.markSeen()`, which stores `onboarding_seen_v1 = true` in `SharedPreferences`, and then open `/intro`. To show the onboarding again to existing users after a big redesign, change the key to `onboarding_seen_v2`.

## Intro screen

`IntroPage` shows the orbit animation (`OrbitHero`), "Welcome to Booqora", and two buttons:

- **Create free account** → `/signup`
- **I already have an account** → `/login`

Below them is the line "14-day free trial · No credit card required". Change it if your plans differ ([Plans & Billing](doc:admin/billing)).
