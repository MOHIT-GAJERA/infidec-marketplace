## Earnings

**Money → Earnings** (`/finance/earnings`, or **More → Earnings** in the Partner app) shows:

- the current **balance** available to withdraw;
- every credit (delivered orders, net of commission and seller coupons) and debit (returns, payouts);
- totals for today, the last 7 days and all time.

The balance is credited automatically when each order is **delivered** ([how settlement works](doc:admin/sellers#commission-and-settlement)).

## Withdrawals

**Money → Withdrawals** (`/finance/withdrawals`) → **Request withdrawal**:

1. Enter the amount (minimum ₹100, up to your balance).
2. Choose **bank**, **UPI** or **cash**; bank and UPI details are pre-filled from your store settings.
3. Submit. The amount is held immediately, and the request shows **pending** until the admin approves, pays or rejects it. A rejection returns the amount to your balance.

## Coupons

**Marketing → Coupons** (`/marketing/coupons`, or **More → Coupons** in the app). Create codes such as `SPICE20` with the same options as platform coupons ([Coupons](doc:admin/coupons)). They apply **only to your products**, and their cost is deducted from your settlement.

## Abandoned carts

**Marketing → Abandoned carts** lists customers who left **your** products in their cart for more than 6 hours, with the cart value, so you can follow up with an offer.

## Reports

| Report | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Sales** (`/reports/sales`) | Your orders and revenue per day, by status, channel and payment method |
| **Product sales** (`/reports/products`) | Your best and slowest products |
| **POS** (`/reports/pos`) | Your counter sales |
