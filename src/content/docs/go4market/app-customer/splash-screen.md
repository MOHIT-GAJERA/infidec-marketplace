The Customer app has two splash stages:

1. **Native splash**: shown by Android while Flutter starts. It is generated by `flutter_native_splash`.
2. **In-app splash**: `lib/screens/splash_screen.dart`, the branded screen that initialises Firebase, loads settings and decides whether to show onboarding, sign-in or home.

![In-app splash](/docs/go4market/images/app-customer-splash.webp "The splash screen: logo centred on a light background")

## Native splash

```yaml title="apps/customer_app/pubspec.yaml"
flutter_native_splash:
  color: "#F3FBF6"
  image: assets/brand/logo.png
  color_dark: "#0B1410"
  image_dark: assets/brand/logo.png
  android_12:
    image: assets/brand/splash_android12.png
    color: "#F3FBF6"
    icon_background_color: "#FFFFFF"
    image_dark: assets/brand/splash_android12.png
    color_dark: "#0B1410"
    icon_background_color_dark: "#FFFFFF"
  android: true
  ios: true
  fullscreen: false
```

Change the colours and images, then regenerate:

```bash
cd apps/customer_app
dart run flutter_native_splash:create
```

:::note Android 12 and newer
Android 12+ always shows the **app icon** style splash: a circle-masked image on a solid colour. That is why there is a separate `splash_android12.png`; keep the logo inside the central circle (about two-thirds of the image).
:::

## In-app splash and onboarding

- `lib/screens/splash_screen.dart`: logo, background doodle (`lib/widgets/doodle.dart`) and timing.
- `lib/screens/onboarding_screen.dart`: the intro slides shown on first launch. Edit the titles and texts in `lib/l10n/app_en.arb` and `app_hi.arb`, or replace the illustrations in the screen file.
