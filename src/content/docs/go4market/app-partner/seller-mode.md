## Dashboard

Today's revenue, number of orders, **balance** and product count, order status tiles (new, confirmed, packed, on the way, delivered, cancelled) and a 7-day sales chart.

## Orders

Tabs: **New, Confirmed, Packed, Ready / Out, Delivered, Cancelled, All**. Open an order to **Accept / Reject**, **Mark as packed**, **Assign delivery partner**, or mark a pickup order **Ready for pickup** and then **delivered with the customer's OTP**. Call the customer from the order. The workflow is described in [Seller Panel → Orders](doc:seller/orders).

## Products and stock

Search by name, brand or **barcode** (camera scan), filter by status (All, Active, Pending, Rejected) and **Add product** with photos from the camera or gallery, variants, add-ons, veg flag, barcode and AI description. **More → Stock** shows low and out-of-stock items for quick restocking.

## POS

![POS](/docs/go4market/images/app-partner-seller-pos.webp "Quick sale: tap products or scan barcodes, then charge cash, card or UPI")

**Quick sale**: tap products or scan barcodes with the camera, adjust quantities, then charge **cash, card or UPI**. The sale is recorded through `posCheckout` as a delivered POS order ([Point of Sale](doc:admin/pos)).

## More

| Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| Stock | Quick restock and low-stock alerts |
| Coupons | Store coupons that apply only to your products |
| Delivery boys | Your own riders (if allowed) and the invitation message ([Own Riders](doc:seller/riders)) |
| Reviews | What customers say about your products |
| Returns | Approve, reject, mark picked and refund |
| Earnings | Balance and credits after commission |
| Withdrawals | Request payouts (bank, UPI, cash) |
| Store settings | Profile, logo, banner, address, map location, bank details |
| Notifications, Help & FAQ, Settings | Inbox, help, dark mode, language, log out |
